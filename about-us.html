About Us

Dang & Associates LLC is a family-owned business committed to providing reliable, personalized tax and accounting services. We understand the unique needs of individuals, families, and small business owners - because we're one ourselves.

Founded in Fredericksburg, Virginia, our firm was built on the belief that trust, transparency, and relationship matter. We take pride in offering year-round support, clear communication, and practical solutions to clients across all 50 states.

As a family-run practice, we treat our clients like family - because to us, every number has a name and every story matters.

Meet Mandy Dang

Founder/Enrolled Agent

Mandy Dang is the heart behind Dang & Associates LLC. She holds a Bachelor of Science in Finance from Old Dominion University and is a federally licensed Enrolled Agent, serving clients nationwide.

Her journey began in Northern Virginia as a co-founder of D&J Group, a successful tax business she later sold before settling in Fredericksburg. Encouraged by the continued loyalty of her clients, Mandy launched Dang & Associates LLC - where she combines her love of numbers with a personal approach to tax and accounting services.

Mandy believes in building relationships first and helping clients feel confident and supported every step of the way.

Meet Carly Nguyen

Carly Nguyen is a dedicated member of the Dang & Associates team, specializing in tax preparation and payroll services. She brings a strong work ethic, attention to detail, and a client-first attitude to every interaction.
